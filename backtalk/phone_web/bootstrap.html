The comparison that actually matters

This page cannot be trusted to tell you the truth about what you downloaded — that's the whole reason this step exists. Instead, look at your phone's own certificate-install screen:
iPhone: after downloading, tap it → tap More Details on the profile screen → find the fingerprint shown there.
Android: the certificate-install dialog shows similar detail before you confirm.
SHA-256 is the ONLY value that counts. Compare that exact value against the SHA-256 fingerprint on the Jarvis desktop screen (or its QR code). A SHA-1 value is sometimes shown too, on either side — it is informational only and must never be used to approve or compare; it is never an acceptable substitute for SHA-256.
If your phone's SHA-256 does not match exactly, or your phone's install screen does not show a SHA-256 fingerprint at all (only SHA-1, or nothing): DO NOT INSTALL THIS CA ON THIS DEVICE. Close this page and tell your Jarvis operator — something on this network may be tampering with the connection, or this device simply can't be verified safely and voice access isn't worth that risk.
